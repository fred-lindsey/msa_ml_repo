### Analytics Project Lifecycle

The process as outlined:

Initiation:  project selection – scope documentation (proposal) – team selection – sponsor guidelines– internal working agreement – sponsor background research – NDA signing - kickoff meeting – transfer the data

Planning:  scope elaboration/refinement – work breakdown structure (product backlog) – scheduling/assignment 

Executing: DO THE ANALYTIC WORK – interim deliverables

Monitoring and Controlling:  weekly progress reports – coaching staff meetings

Closing:  report – presentation – other final deliverables – sponsor feedback
